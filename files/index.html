We are a content production team focused primarily on manga and webtoons, with a strong track record of creating original works. Over the years, we have collaborated with more than 100 creators across the projects we have produced. By fully embracing each creator's unique ability to capture what is truly engaging, we aim to create works that balance both contemporary relevance and timeless appeal.

Just as the Roman Empire once brought cultural stability and prosperity through Pax Romana, we draw inspiration from Japan's creative spirit—Zen—to envision a world where exciting and compelling stories spread across the globe.
